Rekollab Studio

A free Windows app to teach on a whiteboard and record it with your face cam, in one take.

  • Whiteboard with pens, highlighter, shapes and handwriting text
  • Use your webcam, or your iPhone or Android phone as the camera
  • AI background remover and green screen
  • Record the board or any app, with clean AI voice
  • Free video editor, export up to 4K
  • AI Shorts with your own Gemini API key

Sign in with Google. Your recordings stay on your computer.
