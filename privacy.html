Rekollab Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Rekollab Studio is a free Windows app made by Ankit Raj. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version

  • Your recordings, boards and edits stay on your computer. We never receive them.
  • There is no Rekollab server. We do not collect, store, sell or share your data.
  • The app talks to Google when you sign in and when you make AI Shorts with your own Gemini API key. If you use your phone as a camera, the picture may pass through a temporary encrypted Cloudflare link.

Signing in with Google

When you press "Continue with Google", Google's sign-in page opens in your own browser. Rekollab Studio never sees your Google password.

After you sign in, Google gives the app your name, email address and profile picture. The app keeps these only on your computer, encrypted with Windows, so it can show who is signed in and keep you signed in. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Pressing "Sign out" deletes them from your computer.

Your recordings and files

Everything you record, write on the board, edit or export is saved only on your computer, in your Windows user folder and your Downloads folder. Nothing is uploaded by Rekollab Studio.

Camera and microphone

The app uses your camera and microphone only while you use them in the app, to show your face cam and record your voice. If you use your phone as a camera, the video goes from your phone straight to your own computer over your Wi-Fi, or through a temporary encrypted Cloudflare link (Cloudflare only passes it on and does not keep it). We never receive it.

AI Shorts and your Gemini API key

AI Shorts is optional. To use it, you paste your own Google Gemini API key. The key is saved only on your computer.

When you ask the app to find the best moments, the sound of that one video (not the picture) is sent from your computer to Google Gemini using your key. Google handles it under its own terms and privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms. On Google's free tier, Google may use that data to improve its products. You can turn on billing for your key in Google AI Studio if you do not want that.

The shorts themselves are made on your computer.

Children

Rekollab Studio is not made for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
